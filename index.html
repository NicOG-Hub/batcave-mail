batcave-mail

A private, single-user tool.

batcave-mail is a small set of scripts that Nicolás Escobar runs on his own computer to organise his own email, files and documents. It is not a public service: nobody else can sign up for it, and the only Google accounts that use it belong to the developer.

With the owner's permission it can:

  • read selected Gmail messages (for example, bank notifications) to keep a personal expense record;
  • create Gmail drafts that the owner reviews and sends himself;
  • open, upload and update Google Drive files and Google Docs that the owner works on.

Read the privacy policy.
