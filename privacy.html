batcave-mail privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Who uses this app

batcave-mail is a private tool built and used only by its developer, Nicolás Escobar, on his own computer. It is not offered to the public. The only Google accounts that sign in to it are the developer's own.

What Google data it accesses

  • Gmail, read-only: reads selected messages, such as bank and card notifications, to keep the owner's personal expense record.
  • Gmail drafts: creates draft emails. The app never sends mail; the owner reviews and sends every draft himself.
  • Google Drive and Google Docs: opens, uploads and updates files and documents the owner works on.

Where the data goes

  • Data is processed and stored on the owner's own computer. Sign-in tokens are stored only there.
  • The developer runs no server that receives this data.
  • When the owner asks an AI assistant he uses (for example, Anthropic's Claude) to work on his own messages or documents, that content is processed by that assistant only to carry out his request.
  • Data is never sold, never shared for advertising, and never used to train general-purpose AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

batcave-mail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the local records from his computer.
